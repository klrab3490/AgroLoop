Circular Flow Architecture

How The Closed-Loop Nutrient Cycle Works

Three synchronized stages connecting urban organic waste generators with regenerative agricultural soils.

🥦
Stage 01

Households Log Organics

Kitchen scraps, peels, and garden clippings are weighed and photographed. A cryptographically timestamped collection pass is registered in your regional hub.

🚜
Stage 02

Farmers Claim & Verify

Nearby certified growers dispatch pickup routes, scan your doorstep HMAC QR verification pass, and certify net organic weight at the scale.

🪙
Stage 03

Earn & Redeem EcoTokens

The authoritative ledger mints EcoTokens straight to your wallet. Redeem them for living compost, heirloom seeds, orchard fruits, and farm fresh dairy.

Architecture Evolution

12-Phase System Roadmap

Systematic milestone progression from initial domain logic through cross-platform enterprise scale.

1–8

Phases 1–8: Core Platform & Multi-Client Ecosystem

Completed & Live (v1.0.0)

Spring Boot backend, PostgreSQL, JPA inheritance, waste submissions, pickups & verification, EcoTokens ledger, marketplace, Next.js 15 Web Portal, Tauri v2 Desktop Console, and React Native Expo Mobile App.

9

Phase 9: Dynamic Matching & Route Planning

Planned

Polymorphic WasteMatcher strategy interface (distance & capacity), spatial geo-radius matching, automated farmer dispatch, and multi-stop collection route optimization.

10

Phase 10: Ratings, Reviews & Reputation

Planned

Dual-sided 5-star ratings and verified reviews between Households and Farmers, aggregate reliability scores, and public farmer trust profiles.

11

Phase 11: Real-Time Event Streaming & Push Alerts

Planned

Live status tracking via Server-Sent Events (SSE) / WebSockets, instant token minting alerts, and Expo Push Notifications for mobile devices.

12

Phase 12: Cloud Object Storage & Scaled Infrastructure

Planned

Pluggable S3 / MinIO cloud storage provider with pre-signed URLs, Kubernetes Helm charts, and Prometheus/Grafana telemetry.
